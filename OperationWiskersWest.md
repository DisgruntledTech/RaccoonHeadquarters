## 1. Executive Summary

Due to recent human activity—specifically the conversion of the Oak Ridge Dumpster District into a secure, raccoon-resistant luxury residential complex—the **Shadow-Mask Clan** must undergo a coordinated migration. 

This document outlines the strategic relocation plan for all 24 clan members, detailing route planning, scout reports, danger mitigation, food cache logistics, and post-arrival den allocation. The overarching objective is to execute a full relocation within **three night cycles**, maintaining zero casualties and minimal direct human detection.

## 4. Migration Route & Logistics Plan

```
[Oak Ridge District]
       │
       ▼  (Night 1: Culvert Crossing - 1.2 miles)
[The Sub-Station Fence & Drainage Pipe]
       │
       ▼  (Night 2: The Great Bypass - 2.5 miles)
[Willow Creek Railroad Tracks]
       │
       ▼  (Night 3: Final Approach - 1.1 miles)
[Whispering Pines Sector 7B]
```

### Night 1: Crossing the Perimeter (1.2 miles)
* **Departure Time:** 22:30 (Optimal darkness window).
* **Key Obstacle:** 4-lane suburban highway and chain-link fencing.
* **Strategy:** Use the underground storm sewer culvert beneath Highway 42. Vanguard Scouts will enter first to clear for stray cats or high water.

### Night 2: Railway & Suburban Boundary (2.5 miles)
* **Departure Time:** 21:00.
* **Key Obstacle:** Open terrain along the railway track; potential coyotes.
* **Strategy:** Move under cover of brush lines. Nursery Guard will be sandwiched between Vanguard Scouts and Heavy Foragers.

### Night 3: Final Ridge Ascent & Nesting (1.1 miles)
* **Departure Time:** 22:00.
* **Key Obstacle:** Steep rocky ascent along Willow Creek.
* **Strategy:** Arrival at Whispering Pines Sector 7B by 02:00. Initial den selection and scent marking.
